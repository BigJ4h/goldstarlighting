Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find your answer? Call (848) 403-7111.

What is permanent exterior lighting?

It’s an LED lighting system mounted along your roofline, usually in a slim track that matches your trim. It stays up all year. You can run it as soft white light every evening, or switch to colors for holidays and events.

Can permanent lights be used for Christmas?

Yes. Color-capable systems can run Christmas scenes like red and green or classic warm white, so you don’t need to hang lights each year.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the home and how much of the roofline you want lit. We’ll give you a time estimate along with your quote.

Can I customize the lighting colors?

Color-capable systems let you pick everyday whites, holiday colors, or your own color schemes. Most are controlled from a phone app. We’ll go over the exact options during your consultation.

Do you offer Christmas light installation?

Yes. We design and install Christmas lighting for rooflines, trees and landscaping.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, quotes are free. Request one online or give us a call.

What areas do you service?

We work in Monmouth County, NJ. If you’re nearby and not sure, just ask.
